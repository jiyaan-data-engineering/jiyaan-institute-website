What is Google Cloud Platform?

Enterprise cloud services by Google, the same infrastructure that powers Search and Gmail.

GCP is a suite of cloud services including computing, data storage, analytics and machine learning, with tools to manage them. It runs on the same infrastructure Google uses for its own products like Google Search and Gmail.

Yes. The course is mapped to the Google Cloud Professional Data Engineer and Associate Cloud Engineer exam blueprints, including practice tests and exam strategy sessions.

        

        

          Are the classes live or recorded?
          
Classes are live and interactive, with real-time doubt solving. Recordings are shared after every session so you never miss anything and can revise anytime.

        

        

          Do you provide placement assistance?
          
Absolutely. We offer resume building, mock interviews and LinkedIn optimisation, and connect you with our hiring partner network until you land a role.

        

        

          What is the batch size and schedule?
          
We keep batches small for personal attention. Both weekday and weekend batches are available, with flexible online and classroom options.
